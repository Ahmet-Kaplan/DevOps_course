How a round works

  1. Start the clock. You get ten seconds.
  2. Squash the bug. Click or tap it as fast as you can. It jumps every time you hit it, and whenever it gets bored. Keyboard players can press Enter or Space on the focused bug.
  3. Ship the score. Your round is saved when the clock hits zero. The server rejects anything over 200, so the browser console won't help you.

Why “bug”?

On 9 September 1947, operators of the Harvard Mark II computer found a moth trapped in a relay and taped it into their logbook:

1545 Relay #70 Panel F (moth) in relay. First actual case of bug being found.

Engineers had called glitches “bugs” for decades. This one just happened to be literal.
